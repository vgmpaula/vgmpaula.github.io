01 / ABOUT

A curious kind of chemistry

Science is a way of seeing things differently.

I study how materials transform — and how knowledge should transform with them. My work connects chemistry, engineering, computation and sustainability to rethink what happens to polymers after their first life.

From the molecules that determine whether a solvent works to the processes that make recovery practical, I'm interested in the space where fundamental understanding becomes a better material decision.
